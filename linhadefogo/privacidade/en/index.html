Privacy policy — Linha de Fogo

Last updated: 28 November 2026

In short: Linha de Fogo has no accounts and does not ask for your email, age or location. Your coins, finishes, missions and options stay on your device. Data only leaves your device in four cases: (1) when you play online, the game sends our server the name you chose, the room code and your in-game actions, and the server deletes everything when the room closes; (2) chat messages you write go to the players in your room; (3) the game downloads a public file with events and news; (4) in the Android app, Google processes some data for rewarded ads and purchases, as explained below. We do not use our own analytics tools.

1. Who is responsible

The controller is Ana Gonçalves, a self-employed person who publishes games under the name IWO Games ("we"). Contact for any privacy question: apoio@iwogames.pt.

2. What is stored on your device

The browser's or the app's local storage keeps: the name you typed, the language, your options (sensitivity, sound, crosshair, graphics, keys), coins, finishes you bought or earned, the daily reward, missions, your match statistics, the list of players you muted and a random device code used to rejoin the same room if the connection drops. This data is not sent to us (except the name and the random code when you play online). In the Android app it may be included in your Google account's Android backup, if enabled. Clearing the browser data or uninstalling the app deletes it.

3. Playing online

When you create or join a room, the game connects to our game server (hosted on Cloudflare) and uses: the name you chose (shown to players in your room); the room code and the random device code (to bring players together and recognise you if you reconnect); your in-game actions (position, direction, shots, purchases, grenades, team, weapon finish), which the server uses as referee to validate actions, prevent cheating and send the match state to the room. Cloudflare's infrastructure sees your IP address to deliver the connection; our code does not read or store it. The room only exists in the server's memory and is deleted a few minutes after it becomes empty. We keep no match history or profiles. Anyone with the room code can join, so only share it with people you want to play with. The connection is encrypted (WSS). Legal basis: necessary to provide the service you requested (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

4. Chat, mute and report

In a room you can send quick radio phrases or type messages. Messages go through the server to the players in the room (or only your team) and are not stored. You can mute any player and turn typed messages off in Settings. "Report" opens your email app with a message to apoio@iwogames.pt containing the player's name and the room code; if you send it, we receive your email address and what you write, and use it only to handle the report, keeping it for at most 12 months. Do not write personal data in the chat.

5. Events and news

On start the game downloads a small public file (iwogames.pt/linhadefogo/eventos.json). No data about you is sent in that request, but, as with any web page, the site host (GitHub Pages) sees your IP address and device type, under GitHub's privacy policy.

6. Rewarded ads (Android app only)

The computer version on the website has no ads. In the Android app ads are always optional: you only see one if you tap the button (for example to earn coins or double a reward), never during a match, with a daily limit. Ads are served by Google AdMob, whose SDK may collect and share with Google data such as IP address, ad interactions, diagnostics and device identifiers like the advertising ID, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland the app shows Google's certified consent message (IAB TCF) before requesting ads; the legal basis for accessing your device identifier and personalising ads is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can change any time in the game's Settings ("Privacy" button, when required in your region). We ask Google to show only teen-appropriate ads (max rating "T"). You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

7. In-app purchases (Android app only)

The app may offer coin packs, the Founder Pack (Gold finish and coins) and the Linha de Fogo Pass, a monthly auto-renewing subscription. Everything sold is cosmetic or coins for cosmetics: nothing gives an advantage in the game. Payment is handled by Google Play, which shows the price in your currency, with taxes, before you confirm. We never receive or store your card or payment details. The subscription renews every month until you cancel it in Google Play (Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions).

8. Service providers and transfers outside the EU

We use Cloudflare (online game server), GitHub (website, computer version and events file) and Google (AdMob and Google Play, app only). They may process data outside the European Economic Area, including the United States, with the safeguards required by the GDPR (such as standard contractual clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, where applicable).

9. Children

Linha de Fogo is a shooting game and is not intended for children. It is not intended for anyone under 13 and we do not knowingly collect children's data. Please respect the age rating shown in the store. Parents or guardians can contact us at apoio@iwogames.pt.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability, and the right to withdraw consent at any time. Since we do not keep personal data about you, we usually have nothing to look up or delete, except report or support emails you sent us. Data processed by Google is managed through Google's tools and policy. You may also complain to the Portuguese supervisory authority (CNPD). If you live in the United States: we do not sell your personal data; you can limit ad personalisation by resetting or limiting your advertising ID in Android settings.

11. Changes

If the game starts processing data differently, we update this page and the date above before that version is published.
