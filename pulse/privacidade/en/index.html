Privacy policy

PULSE: Blind Duel · IWO Games · Last updated: 19 November 2026

In short: PULSE has no accounts and never asks for your name, email or location. Your progress stays on your phone. Data leaves your phone in only three cases: (1) if you play an Online duel, we send the room code and your moves to our server, with no name and no account, and it is deleted a few minutes after the duel; (2) when it opens, the game downloads a public file with notices from IWO Games; (3) if your version has ads or the optional purchase, Google processes some data, as explained below. We do not use any analytics or advertising tools of our own.

1. Who is responsible

The data controller is Ana Gonçalves, a self-employed person in Portugal, who publishes games under the name IWO Games (“we”).

For any privacy question: apoio@iwogames.pt.

2. What the game stores on your phone

PULSE stores in the app’s local storage, on your device:

  • your progress (Circuit, Ascension, Streak, records, achievements and missions);
  • your Echo, unlocked colours and settings (language, sound, vibration, volume, radar, graphics);
  • game statistics (for example duels played and won), day streaks and daily-challenge results.

This data never leaves your phone. If you uninstall the game or choose “Erase progress” in Options, it is deleted. The “backup” (a text starting with PULSE1.) only exists if you copy it yourself and stays wherever you paste it; we never receive it.

The game language is picked from your phone’s language, locally; that information is not sent anywhere.

3. Permissions

The game uses your phone’s vibration (you can turn it off in Options) and the internet connection, which is only used for the online duel, for IWO Games notices and, where your version has them, for ads and the optional purchase. It does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location or files.

4. Online duel (optional)

The Online duel only starts if you choose it in the menu. To make it work, the game connects to our server (hosted on Cloudflare) and uses this data:

DataWhyHow long it stays
Room code (4 letters) and a random ticket, not linked to youTo connect the two players to the same room and recognise you if your connection drops and returnsWhile the room exists
Your moves (move, sonar, shoot, decoy, wait) and the duel state (positions, round results)The server is the referee: it validates moves and tells each player only what they can see or hearWhile the room exists
IP address of the connectionSeen by Cloudflare’s infrastructure to deliver the connection. Our code does not read or store itAccording to Cloudflare’s policy

There is no account, name, chat or free text: your rival never receives data about you, only the game’s sounds and results. The room and its state are deleted when the duel ends or when the room is inactive (a few minutes). Anyone who has the 4-letter code while the room is waiting can join it, so share the code only with the person you want to play with.

Legal basis: we process this data because it is necessary to provide the service you asked for (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). The connection is encrypted (HTTPS/WSS).

5. IWO Games notices

When it opens, the game may download a small public file (iwogames.pt/pulse/liveops.json) with notices and events. The game sends no data about you in that request, but, as with any web page visit, the server hosting the site (GitHub Pages) sees your IP address and device type, as described in GitHub’s privacy statement. If there is no connection, the game works as usual.

6. Sharing your result or room code

The “Share” and “Share code” buttons create a plain text (for example your result, your day streak or the room code) and open Android’s share menu or, if the phone does not allow it, copy the text to the clipboard. The text only goes to the app where you send or paste it; we do not receive it.

7. Ads (where your version has them)

The game shows optional rewarded ads: you only see one if you choose to, for example to earn more Echo or to save a day streak. If your version allows it, it may also show ads between duels: never in the middle of a duel, with frequency limits, and never to people who have the Supporter Pack. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure them, Google’s SDK may collect and share with Google, for example, your IP address, ad interactions, diagnostic information and device identifiers such as the advertising ID, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention.

  • Consent. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game shows a Google consent message (a certified consent management platform based on the IAB TCF) before requesting ads. The legal basis for accessing your device identifier and personalising ads is your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). If you do not consent, the game may show non-personalised ads or, in some cases, none. You can change your choice at any time in Options → “Privacy and ads” (the button appears when the law requires it in your region).
  • Age. The game is not aimed at under-13s and we ask Google to show only ads suitable for teenagers (maximum content rating “T”).
  • You can reset or delete the advertising ID and limit personalisation in Android settings (Google → Ads).
  • More information: Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Rewarded ads have a daily limit and are always your choice: the game never forces you to watch them.

8. In-app purchases (where your version has them)

The game may offer an optional one-time purchase (“Supporter Pack”: all colours, double Echo, never any ads between duels and ★ on shares). Payment is handled by Google Play, which shows the price in your currency, with taxes, before you confirm. We do not receive or store your card or payment-method details.

Google Play tells the game, on your device, that you own the purchase (for example, to restore it if you reinstall the game or change phone with the same Google account) and, if the purchase is refunded, the Pack stops being active. We do not sell loose Echo: Echo is earned by playing (VIP includes only a small daily Echo bonus). We never sell advantages in duels: purchases are only colours, conveniences and badges.

The game may also offer monthly VIP, an auto-renewing subscription: while active it includes everything in the Supporter Pack, no ads (rewarded ads become free), a monthly pass with daily Echo, a golden VIP badge and a colour and trail exclusive to the month. The price (at launch, €4.99 a month, taxes included) is shown by Google Play, in your currency, before you confirm. Google Play charges your Google account and renews the subscription every month until you cancel; you can cancel at any time in Google Play (Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions) or with the “Manage subscription” button in the game’s Options, and you keep VIP until the end of the period you have already paid for. We do not see or store payment details. Google Play tells the game, on your device, that the subscription is active; if it ends, is cancelled and the paid period runs out, or is refunded, VIP stops being active.

9. Service providers and transfers outside the EU

To run the game we use: Cloudflare (Online duel server), GitHub (hosting of the website and the notices file) and Google (AdMob and Google Play). These companies may process data in countries outside the European Economic Area, including the United States, with the safeguards provided by the GDPR (such as standard contractual clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, where applicable).

10. Children

PULSE is not aimed at children under 13 (the minimum age to consent alone to data processing in Portugal) and we do not knowingly collect data from children. If you are a parent or guardian and think a child has sent us data, write to apoio@iwogames.pt.

11. Data retention and your rights

How long we keep data: your progress, until you uninstall the game or erase the progress; online-duel rooms and moves, only while the room exists (a few minutes); ad-SDK and purchase data, for as long as Google determines in its policy. We do not profile you or make automated decisions about you.

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability, as well as the right to withdraw consent at any time. Because we do not keep personal data about you (your progress stays on your phone and online rooms are deleted shortly after), we normally have nothing to look up or erase on our side; data processed by Google (ads and purchases) is managed through Google’s tools and policy (for example, at myaccount.google.com).

You can always contact us at apoio@iwogames.pt. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Portuguese supervisory authority, the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD), or with the authority in your own country.

If you live in the United States (for example, California): we do not sell your personal data. The advertising identifiers Google uses in ads may be considered “sharing” for personalised advertising in some states; you can opt out by resetting or limiting the advertising ID in Android settings, and you can ask us for information or deletion at apoio@iwogames.pt.

12. Changes to this policy

If the game starts processing data in a different way (for example by adding a new feature), we update this page and the date at the top before that version is released.
