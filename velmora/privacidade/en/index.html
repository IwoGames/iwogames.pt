Data, purposes and legal basis

Game data: progress, chosen character, materials, mounts, summons, purchased cosmetics, settings and language. They stay on your device only; we do not receive them. They are deleted when you uninstall the game or clear the app's data.

Rewarded ads (Google AdMob): advertising ID, IP address, technical device data and interaction with ads, to show and measure ads and prevent fraud. Ads only appear when the player chooses to watch a video to get a reward, and they are limited to content suitable for teenagers. Legal basis: consent for personalised ads, asked when the game opens (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR); legitimate interest for non-personalised ads and fraud prevention (Art. 6(1)(f)). You can change your choice in Shop › Privacy.

Purchases (Google Play and Unity IAP): payment is processed by Google; we only receive the purchase confirmation so we can deliver it. The Unity purchasing library included in the game sends Unity device identifiers, purchase history, approximate location (from the IP address) and technical diagnostics, so that purchases work and for statistics. Legal basis: performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) and legitimate interest in keeping purchases working (Art. 6(1)(f)). Summons in the game can never be bought with money. The VIP subscription renews every month through Google Play and can be cancelled at any time in your Google Play subscriptions (in the game: Shop › Manage subscription).

Camera (augmented reality, optional): if you choose to fight in augmented reality, the camera image is processed on the device only, to place the battle on your table or floor. It is never recorded or sent. You can refuse the permission and play normally.

Playing with friends: co-op connects devices on the same Wi-Fi network directly and only exchanges game data (position, mount, enemies and damage) while the room is open. It does not go through our servers and there is no text or voice chat. The host's network address is only used to connect; the game does not store it. The friend code ("Friend's echo") only contains the chosen character, weapon level, relics and a random installation number; you share it yourself, as you wish.

You are not obliged to provide data: without consent, ads are not personalised; without the data Google Play requires, purchases are not possible. There is no automated decision-making. Google only builds profiles for personalised ads if you consent.

Recipients and transfers

Google (AdMob and Google Play) and Unity Technologies (Unity IAP). These companies also process data outside the European Economic Area, notably in the USA, under the European Commission's adequacy decision (EU-US Data Privacy Framework) or standard contractual clauses; you may ask us for a copy of these safeguards. More: how Google uses data from partner apps.

Security

Game data does not leave the device; what goes to Google and Unity travels over encrypted connections.

Retention

Data on the device stays until you uninstall the game or clear the app's data. Data processed by Google and Unity follows those companies' retention periods.

Children

The game is not intended for children under 13.

Your rights

You may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability of your data, and withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing already carried out. Just write to apoio@iwogames.pt. You may lodge a complaint with the Portuguese data protection authority, CNPD (www.cnpd.pt).
