Privacy Policy · If I Were... (Se eu fosse...)

Last updated: 19 November 2026 · Português (the Portuguese version prevails if the two differ)

In short

  • No account and no email: the game never asks for your real name.
  • The characters are artificial intelligence (AI). What you write in a case goes to our server and to the AI only to generate the reply, and it is not kept after the case.
  • In duels, the server briefly keeps your duel name, league, points and lines. You can delete all of it inside the game, in Settings.
  • Ads only appear if you choose to watch a video for a reward. Personalised ads depend on your choice.
  • We don't sell your data. You can ask us for a copy, a correction or deletion at any time.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The game «If I Were...» («Se eu fosse...») is published by IWO Games, the trading name of Ana Gonçalves, a sole trader in Portugal, who is the data controller. For any privacy question, write to apoio@iwogames.pt.

Artificial intelligence. The characters in cases and duels are generated by artificial intelligence, based on case files written by IWO Games; the game says so on the start screen. What you write is sent over an encrypted connection to the IWO Games server and to the AI provider, Anthropic (Claude models). Anthropic processes these texts only on our behalf and does not use them to train its models. We don't use your texts to identify you or for advertising, and we don't read them unless you ask us for help with a specific case. Please don't write personal data about yourself or others in the cases: the game doesn't need it.

Dictating instead of typing. The game does not use the microphone or ask for that permission. If you dictate with your phone keyboard's microphone (for example, Gboard), your voice is handled by the keyboard, under its maker's rules; the game only receives the text.

Duels. Other players only see your duel name, league, points and the three-sentence summary of how you played; they never see your lines. The summary is written without names or personal data.

Ads. In Europe, the first time you open the game, Google's consent window appears, with the list of advertising partners. You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Ad privacy. In your phone's settings (usually Google › Ads or Privacy › Ads) you can also delete your advertising ID. To learn how Google uses data, read How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

We don't make automated decisions with legal effects on you. Stars, verdicts and points are only game results.

3. Who we share data with

Only with those who help us run the game, and each one only receives what it needs:

  • Anthropic (artificial intelligence): the texts of cases and duels. Anthropic's privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare (game server hosting): the game's requests; the duel database is stored in the European Union. Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • Google (AdMob and Google Play): rewarded ads and purchases. Google's privacy policy.

Some of these services process data outside the European Economic Area, notably in the United States, under the EU-US adequacy decision (Data Privacy Framework) or standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission. You can ask us for a copy of these safeguards. We don't sell your personal data.

4. Security

Data leaving your phone travels over encrypted connections. The key to the artificial intelligence stays on our server and never inside the game. The duel database is in the European Union, and expired data is deleted automatically every day.

5. Your rights

You can ask for access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability of your data, and withdraw consent at any time, without affecting what was done before.

  • Inside the game: in Settings, "Delete my duel data" deletes your duel name, league, points and recordings from the server (the game first tells you what will be lost); "Delete progress" deletes what is on your phone; "Ad privacy" changes your choice about ads.
  • By email: write to apoio@iwogames.pt (for duels, include your duel name, shown on the duels screen). We reply within one month.

You can also complain to the Portuguese data protection authority, CNPD (www.cnpd.pt), or to the data protection authority of your country. If you live outside the European Union (for example, in the United Kingdom, Brazil or the United States), you have the same rights described here.

6. Children

The game is not intended for children under 13. If you are between 13 and 17, read this policy with an adult and ask permission before making purchases.

7. Changes

If we change this policy, we publish the new version on this page with the update date. If the change is important, we also tell you inside the game.
