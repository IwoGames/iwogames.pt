Data controller

Ana Gonçalves (IwoGames.Studio), self-employed in Portugal · iwogames.studio@gmail.com

Data, purposes and legal basis

Game data: player name, player ID, progress, delivered purchases and settings. They stay on your phone only; we do not receive them.

Purchases (Google Play and Unity IAP): payment is processed by Google; we only receive the purchase confirmation, so we can deliver it. The Unity purchasing library included in the game sends Unity device identifiers, purchase history, approximate location (from the IP address) and technical diagnostic data, so that purchases work and for statistics. Legal basis: performance of a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR) and legitimate interest in keeping purchases working (Article 6(1)(f)).

Rewarded ads (Google AdMob): advertising ID, IP address, technical device data and interaction with the ads, to show and measure ads and prevent fraud. Ads only appear when you choose to watch a video to earn a reward. Legal basis: consent for personalised ads, requested the first time you open the game (Article 6(1)(a)); legitimate interest for non-personalised ads and fraud prevention (Article 6(1)(f)). You can change your choice in Settings › Ad privacy.

Support requests: if you write to us, we receive your email address and whatever you send; the message prepared by the game includes your player ID, the game version and your phone model. Legal basis: legitimate interest in replying (Article 6(1)(f)).

You are not obliged to provide any data: without consent, ads are not personalised; without the data Google Play asks for, you cannot make purchases; without an email address, we cannot reply. There is no automated decision-making. Google only builds profiles for personalised ads if you give consent.

Recipients and transfers

Google (Google Play and AdMob) and Unity Technologies (Unity IAP). These companies also process data outside the European Economic Area, in particular in the USA, under the European Commission's adequacy decision (EU-US Data Privacy Framework) or standard contractual clauses; you can ask us for a copy of these safeguards. More information: how Google uses data from partner apps.

Security

Game data never leaves your phone; what goes to Google and Unity travels over encrypted connections.

Retention and deletion

Game data stays on your phone until you delete it in Settings › Delete data, uninstall the game or clear the app's data. Support emails are kept for up to 12 months after the last contact. Data processed by Google and Unity follows those companies' retention periods.

Your rights

You can request access to, rectification, erasure, restriction of and objection to the processing of your data, and data portability, and withdraw your consent at any time without affecting processing already carried out, by writing to iwogames.studio@gmail.com. You can lodge a complaint with the Portuguese data protection authority, the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (www.cnpd.pt).
