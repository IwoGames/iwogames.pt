Privacy policy — Spin Feast

Last updated: 27 November 2026 · Versão em português (the Portuguese text is the original; this is a translation)

This policy explains what data the game Spin Feast (Android, Google Play, and the browser version at iwogames.pt) uses, why, who it is shared with, for how long, and how you can exercise your rights. It applies only to this game.

1. Who is responsible

The game is published on Google Play by IWO Games, a brand of Ana Gonçalves, a self-employed person in Portugal, who is the data controller. Contact for any privacy matter: apoio@iwogames.pt.

2. Summary

  • We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or an account: you play without registering.
  • Your progress is stored only on your phone (or in your browser, in the browser version); we do not receive it.
  • Ads only appear when you choose to watch a video to earn a reward (for example “Profit x2”, the “VIP visit” or the doubled daily reward). There are no ads that pop up by themselves and no banners.
  • Personalised ads depend on your consent, which you can change at any time.
  • We use Google services (AdMob, Google Play and Google Fonts). We do not use analytics or tracking tools from other companies. We do not sell data.
  • The optional reminders are generated on your phone itself, without a server.

3. What data, why, and on what legal basis

The legal bases mentioned are those of the GDPR, Article 6(1).

The game itself

  • Data: progress, coins, stars, gems, boosters, achievements, missions, recipe notebook, promotional codes already used and preferences (language, sound, music, vibration, effects, reminders).
  • Why: to save the game and your choices. They stay on your phone only: we do not receive them.
  • Legal basis: performance of a contract (point (b)).

Rewarded ads — Google AdMob

  • Data: advertising ID, IP address (which allows an approximate location), technical device data (model, system, language), interaction with ads and ad error reports.
  • Why: to show and measure ads, limit their frequency and prevent fraud.
  • Legal basis: consent for personalised ads and for use of the device (point (a)); legitimate interest for non-personalised ads, measurement and fraud prevention (point (f)).
  • Consent: in regions where the law requires it (such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), Google shows a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) the first time you open the game. It lists the advertising partners and lets you choose. You can open it again in Settings, on the row with the 📺 icon (where the law of your region provides for it).

Purchases — Google Play

  • Data: purchase confirmation (product and order ID and, for the “Monthly VIP” subscription, whether it is active).
  • Why: to deliver the gems or the pack you bought and to restore one-time purchases (“Starter pack” and “No ads”) and reactivate “Monthly VIP” when you reinstall the game. Payment is handled by Google; we do not see your payment details. Refunds are requested from Google Play, under its rules.
  • Legal basis: performance of a contract (point (b)); legal and tax obligations (point (c)).

Reminders — notifications on your phone

  • Data: the time of the reminders and your choice to receive them. They stay on your phone only; the game does not use a notification server.
  • Why: to tell you, for example, that Grandma Rosa’s safe is full or that an event is on.
  • Legal basis: consent (point (a)), given when you accept Android’s notification permission. You can withdraw it at any time.

Also: when it opens, the game downloads its typeface from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address and technical device data (legitimate interest in showing the game as designed, point (f)). The game also downloads from our website (iwogames.pt, hosted on GitHub Pages) a small file with the events calendar and promotional codes; that file contains no personal data, but the server receives your IP address and technical data of the request, as with any web page (legitimate interest in keeping the game’s events up to date, point (f)). If Android backup is on, your progress may be copied to your own Google account, according to your phone’s settings; we have no access to that copy.

You are not required to provide data. If you do not consent to personalised ads, the ads you see will not be personalised; depending on your region, Google’s form may also limit or prevent rewarded videos, and the game keeps working in the same way. Without notifications the game works the same; without the data Google Play asks for, purchases are not possible. We do not make automated decisions with legal effects on you.

Browser version (iwogames.pt/spinfeast/jogar)

  • Data: progress and preferences are stored in your own browser (local storage). We do not receive them and you can delete them in your browser settings.
  • No ads and no purchases: the browser version has no real ads, purchases or subscriptions; rewards that need a video in the app are given after a short countdown.
  • Providers: the page is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which may log your IP address in its server logs, and the typeface is downloaded from Google Fonts (Google), which also receives your IP address.
  • Legal basis: legitimate interest in providing and securing the service (point (f)); local storage is strictly necessary to save your game.

4. Who we share it with

Only with Google, which acts as a processor or, for ads, as an independent controller, and with GitHub, which hosts our website. We do not sell personal data to anyone. You can read Google’s and GitHub’s policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data from partner apps (AdMob)
  • Google Fonts and privacy
  • Google Payments privacy notice
  • GitHub General Privacy Statement

We may also disclose data when the law requires it or to respond to a valid request from an authority.

5. Transfers outside the EU

Google and GitHub may process data outside the European Economic Area, notably in the USA. These transfers rely on the European Commission’s adequacy decision (EU-US Data Privacy Framework) or on standard contractual clauses approved by the Commission. You can ask us for information about these safeguards.

6. How long we keep it

  • Game data and reminders on your phone: until you uninstall the game or clear the app’s data.
  • Ads (AdMob): according to Google’s retention periods, set out in its policy. We only see aggregated reports that do not identify players.
  • Purchases: the records kept by Google Play and tax documents for the legal period (in Portugal, 10 years).
  • Emails you send us: up to 3 years after the last contact, or as long as needed to reply and prove that we replied.

7. How to control and delete your data

  • Ad consent: in the game, in Settings, on the row with the 📺 icon, you can review or withdraw consent at any time (where the law of your region provides for it).
  • Advertising ID: in Android’s settings (Privacy › Ads) you can reset or delete it and turn off ad personalisation.
  • Reminders: turn them off in the game, in Settings, or in your phone’s notification settings.
  • Delete everything on your phone: in the game, Settings › Restart (🗑️ icon), or uninstall the game.
  • Data we process ourselves: because the game has no accounts and no server of its own, we do not keep player data. We only have the emails you send us; to delete them, or for any request about your data, write to apoio@iwogames.pt with the subject “Spin Feast – privacy”.

8. Your rights

You may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and portability, and object to processing based on legitimate interest. You may withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing already carried out. We reply within one month (extendable by two more months in complex cases, with notice). If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Portuguese data protection authority, the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (www.cnpd.pt), or to the authority of the country where you live.

Outside the European Union. We apply the same rights worldwide, to the extent that your country’s law provides for them. We do not sell personal data. In some countries (for example, US states with privacy laws) ad personalisation may be regarded as “sharing” data for advertising; you can decline it in Android’s ad settings or, where offered, in the game’s Settings, on the row with the 📺 icon.

9. Security

Game data does not leave your phone. What goes to Google travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access to our Google Play and AdMob accounts is limited to the person responsible.

10. Children

The game is not intended for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children of that age. We do not ask for players’ age. In Portugal, below the age of 13, consent must be given by the holder of parental responsibility (Law no. 58/2019, art. 16); other countries may require a higher age. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game or the law changes. The date at the top shows the version in force. If a change is important, we will tell you in the game.
